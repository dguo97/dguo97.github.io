Bristol Activities

Bristol

On Saturdays, I am a regular attendee of parkrun and currently on my journey towards the 50-volunteer milestone t-shirt.
parkrun is a free, community event where people can walk, jog, run, volunteer, or spectate. It’s a 5k that takes place every Saturday morning. parkrun is positive, welcoming, and inclusive— there’s no time limit, and no one ever finishes last. Everyone is welcome to come along.

On Sundays, I’m part of the amazing Woodlands Church. 🌿 It’s a place where people from all over Bristol come together to explore the adventure of following Jesus. Curious about faith? Come and try Alpha—a relaxed, friendly space to ask questions, share thoughts, and discover more. No pressure, just conversation. ✨

“When we offer the little we have, God can bless it and transform it into something incredible.”
