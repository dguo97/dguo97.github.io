Bristol Activities

🏃‍♂️ Saturdays – Eastville parkrun

On Saturdays, I am a regular attendee of Eastville parkrun and currently on my journey towards the 50-volunteer milestone t-shirt.
Join parkrun

parkrun is a free, community event where people can walk, jog, run, volunteer, or spectate. It’s a 5k that takes place every Saturday morning. parkrun is positive, welcoming, and inclusive— there’s no time limit, and no one ever finishes last. Everyone is welcome to come along.

🌿 Sundays – Woodlands Church

On Sundays, I’m part of the amazing youth team at Woodlands Church.

Woodlands Church is a place where people from all over Bristol come together to explore the adventure of following Jesus.

Curious about faith? Come and try Alpha—a relaxed, friendly space to ask questions, share thoughts, and discover more. No pressure, just conversation. ✨
